# Meme Studio 😂

Make a meme in seconds. Pick an image, type your caption, hit download. That's it.

**100% private.** Every bit of the work happens right in your browser — your
image is never uploaded anywhere. There is no server doing image processing,
no account, no tracking. The little server that runs this app only hands you the
page; your photo never leaves your device.

That's not just a privacy nicety — it's why this tool can serve a million people
at once without breaking a sweat. The heavy lifting happens on each visitor's
own device.

## What you can do

- **Upload your own picture** (or drag-and-drop it onto the canvas)
- Or start from one of the built-in **backdrops** — all original gradients,
  no copyrighted templates
- Add **top** and **bottom** captions in the classic meme style (bold white
  text with a thick black outline)
- Tweak the **text size**, pick a **text color**, toggle **UPPERCASE**
- **Drag a caption** to reposition it anywhere on the image
- **Download a PNG** or **copy the image** straight to your clipboard

## Run it locally

You'll need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (version 18 or newer). No other
dependencies to install.

```bash
npm start
```

Then open **http://localhost:3000** in your browser.
